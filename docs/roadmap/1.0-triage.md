# 1.0 scope triage: what blocks 1.0 and what moves to 1.x

Part of the roadmap: [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md). Path shorthand (`E:`, `C:`, `P:`, vault) is defined there.

This page is a proposal for the owner to mark. It lists every row of section 5.3 that was not MERGED or
DONE on 2026-11-18 and recommends one bucket for each. Nothing here changes the roadmap until the
Decision column is filled; until then section 5.3 stands.

## The rule used

A row blocks 1.0 only if one of these is true. The first three come from the freeze rule in
[post-1.0-plan.md](post-1.0-plan.md): what cannot be changed after 1.0 without a 2.0.

1. **Freeze.** It renames, removes or changes a public name, default, output, exit code, reason or
   stored format, or it adds a refusal of something that works today.
2. **Safety.** It fixes a leak, a wrong result reported as success, or a known advisory in a shipped pin.
3. **Broken today.** A shipped command fails or misleads on the documented path.
4. **Release.** 1.0 cannot be built, signed, published or installed without it.
5. **Nearly done.** Most slices are merged and stopping would leave main half-converted.

Everything else is additive, and by the post-1.0 rule an additive change is a 1.x minor. Internal
cleanup with no user-visible change is a 1.x patch and can merge at any time, before or after 1.0.

Buckets: **1.0**, **1.1+** (a 1.x minor, to be grouped into themed releases), **patch** (any time, never
blocks), **close** (not a feature row; remove from the track).

## After the marks

1. Rows marked 1.0 stay in section 5.3. No row is added to 1.0 after this unless it meets rule 1, 2 or 3.
2. Rows marked 1.1+ move out of section 5.3 into the later list, grouped by feature group.
3. Rows marked patch move to a short maintenance list that never gates a release.
4. Rows marked close are removed; the shipped log already holds their history.
